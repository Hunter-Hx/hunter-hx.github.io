The Vendor

From the collection: Silent Cries And Other Poems

I was walking on the street today
and my favourite maize vendor wasn't in,
He wasn't there today,
He wasn't there yesterday.
It's been a week since last I saw him
The smell of the roasted corn is gone.

I can only guess what happened to him-
Maybe he went back home,
Maybe he got a new job,
Maybe life happened to him,
Maybe he's just tired,
Maybe he's sick...
Or worse, maybe he left us.

And I cannot call him-
he only uses till number
Which means somewhere in his
mpesa messages is my phone number..
And I don't have his.
With the current world uncertainty,
It breaks my heart to know I'll never know what happened to him.
But I hope he's fine. I hope he's fine and winning
By Hunter
