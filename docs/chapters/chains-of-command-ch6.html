Chapter Six

Names Wes

Fiction • 5 min read • Updated Mar 2026

My name is Patricia, Ted’s mother. My life is crashing down and the world has isolated itself from me. It’s hard to believe that my son is going to be executed. It’s hard to imagine my son stripped in a chair as they pass hundreds and thousands of voltages through his body. I’ve seen enough. I’m 56. The good and worse have come my way. It’s time to lay all down to let my son live

.

I have decided to file a petition in court to execute me and let my son walk free. I’m sure he deserves more. Jackline doesn’t know any of this. She’s been trying but I know they won’t listen to her.

I’m leaving my home set. Everything is in place. Today, I’m going to listen to what they have to say.

It’s time to die saving my son’s life.
