Chapter Eleven

Names Wes

Fiction • 5 min read • Updated Apr 2026

My name is Jackline. Jackline Akinyi. My brother calls me Akinyi. He loves that name and I love it too. I’m 14 years old. My only brother is scheduled to die in six days. I can do so little to delay or even cancel his execution. I must try to make sure they don’t kill my only brother. I love him and if they kill him, they kill me. He has always been my joy giver. He’s teasing but smart. He calls me the comedian of the family. I time to fall in love.
